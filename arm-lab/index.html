FROM PIXELS TO PARTS

Meet your real arm.

This playground models three rotating joints and one gripper. A matching physical arm needs four independently controlled servos.

  1. Build & powerUse a regulated servo supply sized for your motors. Connect its ground to Uno GND. Don’t power four servos from the Uno’s 5V pin.
  2. Wire the signalsBase → D3 · Shoulder → D5 · Elbow → D6 · Gripper → D9. Start with one unloaded servo.
  3. Calibrate each jointSet neutral, direction, and safe travel limits. The virtual arm’s angles are not direct servo commands.
  4. Add a USB bridgeA future Web Serial connection in desktop Chrome or Edge can send calibrated targets to the included Uno sketch.
This version is a kinematic simulator. It doesn’t model torque, payload, collisions, or grip contact, and it does not send commands to hardware.
